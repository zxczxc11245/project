Introduction to Programming

description

This course is your first and essential step to learning the language of our time. The "Introduction to Programming" course is designed to take you from the very beginning, focusing on building a programmer's mindset before you even start writing a single line of code. You will learn fundamental concepts common to all programming languages, such as variables, functions, and conditions, and how to translate logical ideas into practical programs.

What will you learn?

  • Write your first piece of code.
  • Understanding how computers think.
  • Problem solving.
  • A solid foundation paves the way for you to learn any programming language.
