Encryption

Description

Welcome to the world of cryptography, the science that holds the secrets of the digital world. In this course, we'll delve into the history of cryptography, from ancient times to the modern era, and explore the algorithms that safeguard our data today. You'll learn how plain text is transformed into unbreakable code, and how the encryption technologies the world relies on to protect privacy and security work.

What will you learn?

  • Understanding the difference between symmetric encryption and asymmetric encryption.
  • Learn about the most popular algorithms and how they work.
  • How to protect passwords and sensitive files from hackers.
